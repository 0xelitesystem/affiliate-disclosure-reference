# What needs disclosing

You need to disclose any material connection between you and a product you recommend, including affiliate commissions, free products, sponsorships, and other compensation. An affiliate relationship, where you earn money when your audience buys through your link, is a material connection that must be disclosed.

## Material connections

A material connection is any relationship that could affect how much weight your audience gives your recommendation, and that they would not expect. Being paid a commission, receiving a free product, being sponsored, or having a financial stake all qualify. The principle is that the audience deserves to know about anything that might bias your endorsement, so they can judge it accordingly. Affiliate income is a textbook material connection.

## Affiliate relationships specifically

An affiliate relationship means you earn a commission when someone buys through your link. Because the commission gives you a financial reason to recommend the product, it is exactly the kind of connection the audience would want to know about. Whether the commission is large or small, the relationship exists and must be disclosed. The size of the payment does not change whether disclosure is required.

## It applies across formats

The duty to disclose applies wherever the recommendation appears: a blog post, a video, a social post, an email, a description. Each format needs its own disclosure, placed where the audience of that format will see it. A disclosure on your website does not cover a recommendation in a video, and a disclosure in one video does not cover the next. The connection must be disclosed in the context where the recommendation is made.

## When in doubt, disclose

If you are unsure whether a relationship counts as material, the safe answer is to disclose it. Disclosure costs little and removes the risk of having failed to reveal something that mattered. Over-disclosing a minor connection is harmless; under-disclosing a material one is a violation and a breach of trust. The default of disclosing whenever there is any compensation or connection keeps you safely on the right side of the rule.
