# The FTC requirement

The FTC endorsement guides require that material connections be disclosed clearly and conspicuously, close to the claim, in language the audience will understand. The disclosure must be hard to miss and easy to understand, not buried, vague, or hidden behind a link.

## Clear and conspicuous

The core standard is that a disclosure must be clear and conspicuous, meaning a reasonable person would notice and understand it. It should stand out, not blend into surrounding text or sit in a place people skip. A disclosure that technically exists but that nobody sees does not satisfy the requirement. The test is practical: would an ordinary member of the audience actually register it before acting on the recommendation.

## Close to the claim

The disclosure should appear near the recommendation it relates to, not separated from it. A reader or viewer encountering the recommendation should encounter the disclosure at the same time, without having to go looking. Placing the disclosure far from the claim, such as only in a site-wide footer while the recommendation is in the body, fails the closeness expectation. Proximity is part of what makes a disclosure effective.

## In plain language

The disclosure has to be understandable to the audience, in plain words rather than legal jargon or vague hints. Telling people plainly that you earn a commission communicates the connection; a cryptic note or an obscure term does not. The goal is genuine understanding, so the wording should leave no doubt about what the connection is. Clarity of language is as important as placement.

## The audience perspective governs

Throughout, the standard is judged from the audience's perspective: would they notice it, would they understand it, would they see it before acting. This is why technicalities do not save a poor disclosure; a disclosure that is present but ineffective from the audience's point of view does not meet the requirement. Designing the disclosure around whether the audience will actually get the message is how you satisfy the rule rather than just gesture at it.
