# Affiliate Disclosure

A plain-language reference on affiliate disclosure: what an affiliate relationship is, why you must disclose it, what the FTC endorsement guides require, where and how to place a disclosure, and the mistakes that turn a disclosure into a violation. Written for creators and operators who use affiliate links.

**Not legal advice.** Disclosure rules vary and change. This explains the common requirements and good practice. It does not tell you whether a specific disclosure satisfies the law for your situation. Confirm with a qualified professional.

## The core idea

An affiliate disclosure tells your audience that you earn a commission when they buy through your links. You must disclose because the FTC requires that material connections, like being paid for a referral, be revealed clearly and conspicuously, close to the recommendation. The disclosure has to be where people will actually see it before they act, in plain language, not buried in a footer or hidden behind a link.

## The stance

This reference treats disclosure as both a legal duty and a trust practice. A clear disclosure costs nothing and protects both your audience and you; a missing or hidden one risks both your reputation and a regulatory problem.
