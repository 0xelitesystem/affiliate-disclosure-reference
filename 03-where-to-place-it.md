# Where to place it

Place the affiliate disclosure close to the recommendation and before the audience acts on it: near the link in text, early in a video and where relevant on screen, and in the context where each recommendation appears. It must be visible without the audience having to hunt for it.

## Before the click

The disclosure should reach the audience before they click an affiliate link, not after. A disclosure that appears only after someone has acted has failed its purpose, because the point is to inform the decision. Placing it ahead of or alongside the recommendation ensures the audience knows about the connection while they are deciding, which is when it matters. Timing is part of placement.

## In text

In written content, place the disclosure near the affiliate links, ideally before them or at the start of the content where the links appear, not solely in a footer or a separate page. A reader should see the disclosure as they encounter the recommendations. A single line near the top stating that the content contains affiliate links, plus context near specific links, keeps the disclosure close to the claims it covers.

## In video and audio

In video, disclose early and in a way the audience will catch, both spoken and, where relevant, on screen, rather than only in the description. Many viewers never read the description, so a description-only disclosure can miss them. Stating the affiliate relationship verbally near the start, and reinforcing it on screen when products are recommended, reaches the audience in the medium they are actually consuming. The description is a supplement, not the whole disclosure.

## Per context, every time

Because the disclosure must be close to each recommendation, it has to be repeated in each context where recommendations appear, not made once and assumed to cover everything. Each post, video, and email that contains affiliate links needs its own disclosure. This repetition can feel redundant, but it is what keeps the disclosure close to the claim in every place the claim is made. One disclosure does not travel across all your content.
